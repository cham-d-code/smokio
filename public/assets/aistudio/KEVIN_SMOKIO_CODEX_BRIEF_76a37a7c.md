## 1. Project objective

Create a premium, visually bold, mobile-first website for **Smokio / Kevin Smokio**, a Sri Lankan rapper, singer, lyricist and performer associated with Sinhala hip-hop. The site should introduce his identity, highlight music and videos, guide visitors to official platforms, showcase imagery, and provide a future-ready area for shows and booking enquiries. The visual direction is **cinematic, underground, urban, editorial and modern**—not a generic music template.

**Essential rule:** The information below distinguishes verified/publicly supported material from design copy and placeholders. Use the approved copy, do not fabricate touring dates, awards, streaming counts, testimonials, contact details, age, or a personal history. [S1][S2][S3]

## 4. Recommended page architecture

**Single-page MVP** with clean URL anchors, plus optional dedicated `/music`, `/videos` and `/press` routes in a later phase.

1. **Home / Hero** — oversized artist name, powerful portrait/video still, subtitle and CTAs.
2. **Latest Drop** — *ASAWEN* spotlight, embedded official YouTube video and short release details.
3. **About** — artist story and compact milestones; optional timeline.
4. **Music** — six featured tracks, album/project links and Spotify CTA.
5. **Videos** — responsive YouTube video cards, filtering by featured/recent.
6. **Gallery** — licensed artist photos and performance visuals (initial placeholders).
7. **Shows** — upcoming concerts with **“No dates announced yet”** empty state until verified.
8. **Connect / Booking** — social links, request form with optional email integration after receiving permission.
9. **Footer** — navigation, socials, copyright notice and an accurate site attribution.

Navigation: **SMOKIO** wordmark | Home · About · Music · Videos · Gallery · Shows · Contact | [LISTEN NOW]

## 8. Visual art direction (PROPOSAL, not verified brand guideline)

**Design concept:** Urban editorial meets cinematic music-video treatment: large condensed type, black backgrounds, strong motion, texture, unapologetic whitespace and short copy.

**Proposed palette**

| Token | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Void Black | `#0A0A0A` | Main background |
| Onyx | `#151515` | Cards / raised surfaces |
| Soft White | `#F4F1EA` | Headlines and primary content |
| Signal Orange | `#EF4B23` | Primary accent, button, active state |
| Smoke Gray | `#9B9B9B` | Supporting text |
| Divider | `#2B2B2B` | Borders / rules |

**Fonts:** `Anton` or `Bebas Neue` for oversized display headings; `Montserrat` or `Inter` for body/UI. Use responsibly licensed web fonts and allow Sinhala-capable fallback (`Noto Sans Sinhala`).

**Hero composition:** Black/dark backdrop, edge-lit artist portrait on the right, enormous stacked “SMOKIO” typography on the left, animated muted grain or stripes in background, and bright accent CTA. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Do **not** autoplay audio.

**Creative touches:** Vertical side labels, orange underlined micro-headings, track cover cards with strong shadow overlays, thin separator lines, subtle parallax on desktop, video thumbnails with giant play indicators, editorial timeline, tasteful hover distortions only if accessible.

**Mobile:** Single-column hero, sticky condensed menu, native-feeling swipeable music cards, 44px minimum tap targets, small video preview thumbnails, no heavy scroll-jacking.

## 9. Technical build specification for Codex

**Recommended stack:** Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS; optional Framer Motion / Motion library for light animation; local content arrays for MVP; deploy to Vercel or another suitable platform. Use installed versions and a supported production release. Avoid unnecessary backend complexity until the contact form is connected.

### Functional requirements

- Responsive layout for 360px–wide mobile through large desktop.
- Accessible navigation, visible focus styles, `aria-label` for icon-only controls.
- Feature the latest video prominently; artist videos open **real official YouTube links** / embeds.
- Music tiles have thumbnail, year, track title and `Watch` action; streaming button uses confirmed links only.
- Dynamic arrays for releases and performances; no hardcoded fake metrics.
- Shows section defaults to an honest empty state until events are provided.
- Contact form has client + server validation, spam protection, and **must not show a success state without working delivery**.
- SEO title/meta description, Open Graph preview, Twitter card, canonical URL once domain is known, sitemap/robots only after domain selected.
- JSON-LD `Person` / `MusicGroup`-like schema only when identity/ownership is accurately modeled; do not invent `sameAs` accounts.
- Lazy images, optimized video loading, zero autoplay audio, reduced-motion support.
- Use `next/image` on images that are stored/licensed locally; do not hotlink Spotify CDN in production without authorization.
- Semantic HTML, keyboard usability, adequate contrast, clear breakpoints.
